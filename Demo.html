Why voice matters here
Voice removes the literacy barrier and the keyboard barrier at once. Swahili keyboards are awkward on most handsets and speaking is simply faster — so the voice note is not a fallback. It is the primary input across the region.

Honest limitation
Swahili recognition is solid on standard Kiswahili but degrades on Sheng, coastal variants and heavy code-switching — the registers people actually speak in Nairobi and Dar. Where confidence is low the platform does not guess: it asks one short clarifying question. Closing this gap is one of the areas where deeper Meta ecosystem integration could accelerate the product.
